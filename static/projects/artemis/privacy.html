Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Artemis application

Artemis ("the app") is a personal job-search assistant built and operated by Srijan Singh. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets: to create and update the "Artemis DB" spreadsheet that stores your job searches, job listings, application status and settings.
  • Google Drive: to create the "Artemis" folder, read the resume files you place in it, and save generated documents (apply kits) there.
  • Google Apps Script: to install the Artemis menu and side panels inside the Artemis spreadsheet.
  • Account email address: to confirm the app is acting on the intended Google account.

The app does not read your other spreadsheets, documents or files for any purpose other than the ones listed above, and does not access Gmail, Contacts or Calendar.

How the data is used

  • Your resume text and job descriptions are processed to extract experience, skills and role details, to rank jobs, and to draft application answers.
  • This processing runs on the operator's own computer. Resume text, job descriptions and your saved answers are sent to the Anthropic Claude API solely to perform that processing. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train its models by default.
  • Results are written back to your own Google Sheet and Drive.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except the processor named above for the purpose described. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

Artemis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

  • Your data lives in your own Google Drive and Sheets. You can edit or delete it at any time.
  • A local cache of job postings and extracted fields is kept on the operator's computer and is deleted when no longer needed. Resume contents are not written to logs.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never shared.

Security

Credentials are kept out of source control, all API traffic uses HTTPS, and the app requests only the access listed above.

Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Artemis" folder in your Drive to remove all app data stored there.
  • Email the address below to request deletion of any locally cached data.

Children

Artemis is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
